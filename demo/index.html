Track graph, dispatcher and railnet built from OpenStreetMap rail data. In the track view, coloured track is under a train’s authority, red blocks are reserved for the train approaching them, and each train’s next signal is shown by aspect (a dashed ring means a diverging route). The railnet view shows lines by operator, with double track drawn thicker.
